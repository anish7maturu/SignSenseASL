Only the letters A–Z are signed; numbers and punctuation are skipped. Repeated letters get a small bounce, and J and Z are drawn in the air. Signers use whole-word signs for most everyday words; fingerspelling is mostly for names and new words.

SignSense is a student-built tool for practicing the ASL fingerspelling alphabet. It finds 21 points on your hand with MediaPipe Hands and checks each letter with simple rules, so it can make mistakes, especially with T, N and M. ASL also uses movement, facial expression, grammar and context, which SignSense doesn't check. For real fluency, learn from Deaf teachers and the Deaf community.

Privacy

Your camera

Camera video is processed inside your browser and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your account and progress

Usernames, progress and settings are stored only in this browser (localStorage). There is no server and no email. Passwords are never stored: only a scrambled hash of each one. Clearing your browser's site data deletes everything.

Outside services

The page loads the MediaPipe hand tracker from cdn.jsdelivr.net and its font from Google Fonts. Those services can see that your browser asked for those files, as with any website. SignSense has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Kids

SignSense doesn't ask for names, emails or ages. Pick a username that isn't your real name if you like.
