Only the letters A–Z are signed; numbers and punctuation are skipped. Repeated letters get a small bounce, and J and Z are drawn in the air. Signers use whole-word signs for most everyday words; fingerspelling is mostly for names and new words.

SignSense is an educational prototype, not a certified ASL assessment. It uses MediaPipe Hands (21 hand landmarks) and simplified heuristics for fingerspelling handshapes; J and Z are checked by tracing their movement. ASL includes movement, grammar, facial expression, and context. The translator works with fingerspelling (letters), not whole ASL signs or grammar, and does not verify all sign details. Letters marked 🧪 are harder for a webcam to see. Internet is needed to load MediaPipe; webcam access works on localhost or HTTPS. Progress is saved locally in this browser.
