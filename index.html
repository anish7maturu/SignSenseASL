Units unlock as you go. Teachers and returning signers can open everything at once.

SignSense is an educational prototype, not a certified ASL assessment. It uses MediaPipe Hands (21 hand landmarks) and simplified heuristics for fingerspelling handshapes; J and Z are checked by tracing their movement. ASL includes movement, grammar, facial expression, and context. It does not translate ASL or verify all sign details. Letters marked 🧪 are harder for a webcam to see. Internet is needed to load MediaPipe; webcam access works on localhost or HTTPS. Progress is saved locally in this browser.
