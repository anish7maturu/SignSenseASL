**Description:**
SignSense translates and teaches ASL fingerspelling, the 26 hand letters of American Sign Language.

TRANSLATE
• Sign to Text: fingerspell to your camera and SignSense types each letter live, with captions on the video. Read it aloud or copy it.
• Text to Sign: type a word and an animated 3D hand fingerspells it, letter by letter.
• 12 languages: type in Spanish, French, Chinese, Hindi and more. Your words are translated to English, then fingerspelled.
• Photo to signs: take a picture of a sign or a menu, and SignSense reads the text and signs it.

LEARN
• Teach me: say "Teach me hello" and Finn walks you through every letter, then checks you sign it back.
• Live practice: the camera shows which fingers are right and which to fix.
• Lessons for all 26 letters, a quiz, and six test levels, from single letters to a short conversation.
• XP, streaks and achievements keep you practicing.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Camera video and photos stay on your iPhone and are never recorded or uploaded.
• No ads, no tracking, no email sign-up. Profiles stay on your device, and you can delete yours at any time.

Please note: SignSense recognizes fingerspelling, not whole-word ASL signs or ASL grammar. It can make mistakes, especially with T, N and M. It's a learning tool, not a replacement for an interpreter. Learn from Deaf teachers and the Deaf community for real fluency.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers
Data collected by the developer: **None**. Choose "Data Not Collected."
Why: SignSense has no server. Camera, photos, profiles and progress never leave the device.
Text typed in another language may go to the MyMemory translation service, and speech recognition is done by Apple; neither is collected by the developer. (If Apple's reviewer asks, explain both, as in the privacy policy.)

## Notes for the App Review team
SignSense is an ASL fingerspelling translator and trainer. No account or login is needed: tap "Continue as guest". To try the camera: open Sign to Text, tap "Turn on camera", and hold up a hand. To try voice: open Learn > Teach me and tap the microphone. Profiles are stored only on the device and can be deleted on the Progress page ("Delete my profile").
