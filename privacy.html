SignSense privacy policy

For the SignSense website and the SignSense iPhone app. Last updated November 2026.

Short version: SignSense has no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your camera video, photos, profile and progress stay on your device. The only things that ever leave your device are text you choose to translate from another language, and your voice if you use the microphone button. Both are explained below.

What SignSense uses, and where it goes

DataUsed forLeaves your device?
Camera videoReading your fingerspellingNo. It is processed on the device and never recorded or saved.
Photos you chooseReading text to sign itNo. Text is read on the device.
Username, password, progressSaving your progressNo. Saved only on this device. Passwords are never stored, only a salted, scrambled hash of each one.
Voice (microphone button)Understanding "Teach me hello"Yes, possibly. Speech recognition is done by your browser or by Apple, and Apple or Google may process the audio on their servers. SignSense never records or keeps it.
Text in another languageTranslating it to EnglishSometimes. If your browser has a built-in translator, the text stays on the device. Otherwise it is sent to the free MyMemory service (mymemory.translated.net). Don't translate private information.

Profiles

Profiles don't use email, real names or ages. Pick a username that isn't your real name if you like. You can delete your profile and all of its progress at any time: open the Progress page and choose Delete my profile. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's data for the website, also deletes everything.

Outside services

The website downloads its hand tracker, text reader and font from cdn.jsdelivr.net and Google Fonts. Like any website, those services can see that your device asked for those files. The iPhone app has all of these built in and doesn't download them.

Children

SignSense is made for learners of all ages. It doesn't ask for or collect personal information, so it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. The voice and translation features work as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions

SignSense is an open-source student project. You can ask questions or report a problem on GitHub.
